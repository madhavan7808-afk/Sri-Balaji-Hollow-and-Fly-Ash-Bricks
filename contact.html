Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from architects, civil contractors, and home builders.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for delivery?
We accept orders starting from 1,000 bricks or 300 solid blocks. For bulk orders of 10,000+ units or multi-truck commercial deliveries, special discounted tiered pricing is automatically applied.
Do you provide physical material samples for quality testing?
Yes! We provide complimentary samples of our solid blocks and fly ash bricks so your civil engineer or testing lab can independently verify compressive strength, water absorption, and dimensions.
How fast can readymade compound walls be installed?
Our skilled installation team can erect up to 80 to 100 running feet of precast compound wall per day! It requires no plastering or on-site curing, saving up to 70% in project time compared to traditional brick boundary walls.
Which districts and areas do you deliver to?
We regularly deliver across Salem, Vazhapadi, Attur, Rasipuram, Namakkal, Dharmapuri, and adjacent regions in Tamil Nadu. Our dedicated logistics team ensures on-time arrival with zero-chipping pallet handling.
What payment terms do you support?
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), NEFT/RTGS bank transfers, and cash payments. For ongoing commercial contractor projects, milestone payment terms can be scheduled upon agreement.
