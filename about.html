Our Story

Pioneering Modern Masonry Standards in Salem

Headquartered in Ponnarampatty, Vazhapadi, Sri Balaji Hollow and Fly Ash Bricks has established itself as one of the region's most reputable manufacturers of heavy-duty solid concrete blocks, eco-sustainable fly ash bricks, and readymade precast boundary walls.

Recognizing the recurring issues builders faced with conventional red bricks — such as dimensional unevenness, high water absorption, excessive plastering costs, and supply shortages — we invested in automated high-tonnage hydraulic press technology. Today, our products power residential homes, commercial complexes, and industrial sites across Salem, Attur, and neighboring districts.

Engineering Process

The Science Behind Our Strength

From calibrated raw aggregates to automated water curing, every stage is quality verified.

1. Aggregate Grading

Clean river/M-sand and 6mm gravel are precision-sieved to remove impurities and ensure ideal particle packing density.

2. Pan-Mixer Homogenizing

High-torque planetary pan mixers blend 53-grade OPC cement, fly ash, and specialized hardening admixtures uniformly.

3. 30T Hydraulic Pressing

Heavy hydraulic cylinders compact the mixture inside CNC-cut hardened steel molds, removing microscopic internal air pockets.

4. 21-Day Sprinkler Curing

Molded units are transferred to dedicated shaded bays with automated oscillating water sprinklers for full hydration strength.

5. Batch Compression Testing

Random sample units from every production run undergo hydraulic crushing tests to verify load-bearing capacity exceeds IS standards.

6. Palletized Safe Dispatch

Carefully stacked and strapped on transport lorries for zero edge-chipping delivery straight to your construction job site.
